Can you guess what your friends are thinking?

One friend secretly sees a spot on the board and gives one clue. Everybody else draws a dot where they think it is. Closest dot wins!

try it!
Ari's clue:

tap or drag the dot, then reveal

How to play

1

Peek

The psychic secretly sees the target on a board like Funny ↕ Unfunny and Ugly ↔ Cute.

2

Clue

They say ONE thing that fits. "A pug in a hot dog costume" = cute + funny?

3

Dot!

Everyone draws a dot in secret. Reveal! Bullseye = 4 points.

Why it's fun

✎ Everyone for themselves

No teams. Your guess, your points.

✎ Works on phones

No app, no sign-up. Just open the link.

✎ Endless cards

Mix any two axes, or make up your own!

✎ 4-letter rooms

Text the code to friends, done.

Okay, let's play!

Start a room and send the code to your friends.
