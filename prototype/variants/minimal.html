How it works

Thirty seconds to learn. Hours of arguing.

01

The psychic peeks

One player sees a hidden point on a board with two axes, like Funny–Unfunny and Ugly–Cute.

02

One clue

They say a single thing that belongs at that spot. Nothing more.

03

Everyone guesses

Each player places a dot in secret. Closest to the point scores most.
