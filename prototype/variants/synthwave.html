HOW TO PLAY

01

The psychic peeks

One player secretly sees a target on a board with four labels, like Funny ↕ Unfunny and Ugly ↔ Cute.

02

One clue

They give a single clue that fits that spot. "A pug in a hot dog costume" is pretty cute and kind of funny.

03

Drop your dot

Everyone places a dot in secret. Reveal! A bullseye is 4 points, and the highest score wins.
