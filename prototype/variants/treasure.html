The Pirate's Code

Three rules, sworn on the captain's hat.

⚓ ✦ ⚓
🗺️
THE FIRST RULE

The captain peeks

One player secretly sees where the treasure lies on a map with two pairs of opposites, like Funny ↕ Unfunny and Ugly ↔ Cute.

🦜
THE SECOND RULE

One riddle

They squawk a single clue that belongs at that spot. No more, or it's the plank.

⛏️
THE THIRD RULE

Every pirate digs

Each crewmate marks a spot in secret. Then the treasure's revealed. Dig right on it for 4 gold.
