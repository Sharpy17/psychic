One of you has seen it. Can you find it?

Psychic Plane is a party guessing game. Each round, one player, the psychic, secretly sees a point on a board between two pairs of opposites. They give one clue. Everyone else marks where they think the point is. The closest guess takes the points.

  • free to play
  • no downloads
  • works on phones

The method

Three steps. One connection.

Every round follows the same thread, from the psychic's secret to the closest guess.

Step 1

The psychic peeks

One player sees where the hidden point sits between two pairs of opposites, like funny / unfunny and ugly / cute.

Step 2

One clue

They say one thing that belongs at that spot. "A pug in a hot dog costume." Nothing more.

Step 3

Everyone marks it

Each player places a dot in secret. Then the point is revealed:

  • 4bullseye
  • 3very close
  • 2in the zone
