How to play

Seriously, it takes 30 seconds.

1

Peek!

The psychic secretly sees where the target is, on a board like Funny ↕ Unfunny and Ugly ↔ Cute.

2

Clue!

They say ONE thing that fits that spot. "A pug in a hot dog costume" is cute AND funny, right?

3

Drop!

Everyone drops a dot in secret. Then the big reveal! Bullseye = 4 points.
