Three steps to a direct hit

Only they can see where the contact sits on a grid with two axes, like Funny ↕ Unfunny and Ugly ↔ Cute.

02 / TRANSMIT

One clue goes out

A single word or phrase that belongs at those coordinates. No follow-up transmissions.

03 / TRIANGULATE

Everyone places a fix

Each operator drops a dot in secret. Ping! The contact is revealed. Direct hit = 4 points.
