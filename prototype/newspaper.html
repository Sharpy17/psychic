Local Friends Can Read Each Other's Minds, Witnesses Claim

One psychic, one hidden target, one clue. Experts say the results are “frankly alarming” and “a great way to spend a Friday.”

By Our Psychic Correspondent Filed from your living room

Reports reached this paper late last night of a new parlour game sweeping group chats and game nights alike. Players gather around a single board bearing four words, one at each edge, and attempt the impossible: guessing exactly what their friends are thinking.

Each round, one player is named the psychic and shown a secret target. They offer a single clue. The rest, working alone and in silence, place a dot where they believe the target lies.

“How is a goose a nine on scary? Explain yourself.”

When every dot is locked, all are revealed at once. Bullseyes are rewarded, wild misses are remembered for years, and friendships are, sources confirm, mostly preserved.

The game requires no download and runs on any phone. Readers are advised to try today's puzzle, printed at right.

How to Play

Page A1 · Round begins

Psychic Peeks at Secret Target

Each round, one player privately sees where the target sits on a board with four labels, such as Funny / Unfunny and Ugly / Cute. Everyone gets a turn as the psychic.

Page A2 · The clue

Single Clue Given; Room Baffled

The psychic offers one line that lands on the spot. “A pug in a hot dog costume” is very cute and somewhat funny... or is it? Debate is encouraged.

Page A3 · The reveal

Dots Placed in Secret, Scores Tallied

Everyone drops a dot without peeking. Then every guess appears at once, the target rings light up, and points are awarded. Highest total after the last round wins.
