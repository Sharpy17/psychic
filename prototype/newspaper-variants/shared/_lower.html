Rules of playHow the Game Is Played

I. The Psychic Takes a Peek

One player privately sees where the target sits between two pairs of opposing words. Nobody else may look.

II. A Single Clue Is Given

Something that belongs at that exact spot. A pug in a hot dog costume, for instance: rather cute, and somewhat funny.

III. Readers Place Their Marks

Every other player marks the board in secret. Then all is revealed. A bullseye earns four points; the highest total wins.
