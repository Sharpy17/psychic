How They Did It, Step by Step

By our Chief Mind-Reading Reporter

Each round, one player, the “psychic”, is shown a secret point on a board marked with two pairs of opposites, such as Funny and Unfunny, or Ugly and Cute.

The psychic may offer just one clue. The remaining players then mark, in secret, where they believe the point lies. When the target is revealed, the closest guesses earn the most points.

“It took thirty seconds to learn,” one stunned guest told us, “and three hours to stop arguing about whether a goose is scary.”
