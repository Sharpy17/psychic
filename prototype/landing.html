How to play

Takes 30 seconds to learn, and a few minutes before someone is yelling "how is that a 9?!"

The psychic peeks

Each round one player secretly sees where the target sits on a board with four labels, like Funny ↕ Unfunny and Ugly ↔ Cute.

They give one clue

Something that lands on that spot. "A pug in a hot dog costume" is pretty cute and kind of funny... right?

Everyone places a dot

In secret. Then the reveal shows every dot at once. Bullseye is 4 points, and the highest score wins.

Built for game night

Everyone joins from their own device. No accounts, no app store.

⚡

Every player for themselves

No teams. Everyone guesses in secret, and live scores sit right beside the board.

📱

Phone-friendly

Big touch targets and a board that's easy to tap and drag with a thumb.

🃏

Endless combos
