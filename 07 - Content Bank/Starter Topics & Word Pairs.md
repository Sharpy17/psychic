## 💡 Content Expansion Guidelines
* **Avoid Obvious Synonyms:** Ensure negative and positive ends are distinct opposites.
* **Culturally Universal:** Try to keep topics broad so players don't need niche trivia knowledge to have fun.
* **Humor Factor:** Subjective spectrums (e.g. *Dies First in Horror Movie* vs *Survives Apocalypse*) generate much better debates than purely objective metrics (e.g. *Short* vs *Tall*).
