How to enter

Each round, one player is the psychic and sees a secret spot on a chart with two axes. The psychic gives a single clue, and everyone else marks where they think it lies.

Nobody learns a thing. No target, no score, not a hint. After the fourth round, you cut out your entry, fold it, seal it and put it in the mail.

Then you wait.

The Contest Rules

  1. The psychic sees the target. It’s a secret spot on a chart with two labelled axes.
  2. The psychic gives one clue. Just one, and it has to fit both axes at once.
  3. Everyone else marks the chart. Time is limited, and nobody sees the target or a score.
  4. The next player becomes the psychic. After 4 rounds, everyone clips their entry and mails it.
  5. The reply arrives. Only now do you see every target, every result and who won.

A word to the psychic

Your score is the average of everyone else’s, so a clever clue that only you understand costs you too. And you won’t know whether it worked until the mail comes.
