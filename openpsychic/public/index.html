By our Contest Desk, from the front row · Entries close after round four

Ladies, gentlemen and undecided parties: the circus is coming, and one seat is up for grabs. To win it, you must read your friends’ minds by mail, head to head, free for all or in teams.

Each round, one brave performer, the “psychic”, is shown a secret point on a board marked with two pairs of opposites, and offers a single clue. The guessers mark where they believe the point lies. Nobody sees an answer.

After four rounds, every entry is clipped, sealed and mailed. Then you wait. The reply shows every clue, every target and every mark, with a leaderboard at the end. The top name takes the ticket.

“It took thirty seconds to learn,” said one player, “and three hours to stop arguing about whether a goose is scary.” No downloads or accounts are required.

Rules of playHow the Game Is Played

I. The Psychic Gives One Clue

A psychic privately sees where the target sits between two pairs of opposing words, then offers a single clue. Who plays psychic depends on the ring you choose.

II. Four Rounds, No Answers

The guessers mark the board within 60 seconds. No target, no score, not a hint. After round four, all the marks go in the mail together.

III. The Reply Names a Winner

It shows every clue, every target and every mark. A bullseye earns 1,000 points. The top of the leaderboard wins the ticket to the circus, with one each for a winning team.

A Three-Ring Circus

Ring one

Head to Head

2 players

You are both psychic and guesser. You each write a clue at the same moment, swap, then each guess the other’s clue at the same moment. You score for how close your own guess lands.

Ring two

Free for All

3 to 8 players

A psychic is drawn at random each round, and everyone else guesses the same clue. Nobody goes twice until everyone has had a turn. The psychic earns the average of the others’ scores, plus 250 if two or more hit the Bullseye.

Ring three

Team Against Team

4 or more, at least 2 on each team

Both teams chase the same secret target, each with its own psychic, and both clues are in plain view. Write something only your team will get, like an inside joke. Make it too obvious and the other team can use it too. Each team moves one marker together, everyone seeing it live, and locks it in when all agree.
